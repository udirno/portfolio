## The Problem

Most digital greeting card tools are either outdated (clip-art aesthetics, account walls) or Western-centric — they cover Christmas and birthdays but ignore Diwali, Eid, Lunar New Year, or Nowruz. And the ones that do exist rarely produce cards you'd actually want to send.

## The Solution

Cardly generates personalized greeting cards in seconds. Pick an occasion, set the vibe (warm, funny, elegant, bold), and Claude writes copy that avoids generic greeting-card language. The card renders on one of 30 hand-built SVG/CSS templates across 15 occasions — each with its own color palette and layered decorative elements.

Cards are editable inline, exportable as PNG at 2x resolution with embedded fonts, and shareable via link with a 30-day TTL backed by Supabase. No account needed.

## Key Features

- **30 templates across 15 occasions** — cultural-first: Diwali, Eid, Lunar New Year, Nowruz, Hanukkah alongside birthdays and weddings
- **Prompt-engineered tone control** — forbidden cliché list, vibe calibration, and cultural specificity baked into the system prompt
- **PNG export pipeline** — html-to-image with custom font embedding at 2x pixel ratio
- **Shareable links** — Supabase-backed with RLS-enforced 30-day TTL, nanoid slugs
- **Zero friction** — no accounts, no email, instant generation
